Behind the Lens

Hi! I'm Morris, a portrait and lifestyle photographer based in Fremont, California. I started fiddling with a camera in 2016, and over the years my loose hobby became a professional passion.

While I specialize in portraits, photography has taken me everywhere — from picturesque college campuses to famous boulevards, dense forests, and cliffs in Southeast Asia. I love establishing connections with the people I capture. If you have a story to tell, let's connect!
